About the College

A community focused on education, skills and personal development.

Education with purpose

Pune District Education Association's Baburaoji Gholap College is located in Sangvi, Pune. Established in June 1989, the college was founded to expand access to higher education for communities in and around the rapidly developing areas of Sangvi and nearby localities.

The college offers programmes across Arts, Commerce, Science and vocational education. Its academic activities include teaching, research and opportunities for students to develop practical and professional skills.

The college is affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University. For the latest admissions, programme details and notices, please consult the official college website.

About Baburaoji Gholap College

College background, academic opportunities and student development.

Pune District Education Association (P.D.E.A.), Pune, was established in 1941 by social reformer Hon. Baburaoji Gholap with the vision “Bahujan Hitay, Bahujan Sukhay”. The association received the Government of Maharashtra's Best Educational Institution Award in 2000.

P.D.E.A.'s Baburaoji Gholap College, Sangvi, Pune, was established in June 1989 with the motivation of Late Prof. Ramakrishna More. It was created to improve access to higher education for students in Sangvi and nearby areas, including Aundh, Baner, Wakad, Kalewadi, Pimple Gurav, Pimple Nilakh and Thergaon.

The college serves rural, semi-urban and urban communities. Its facilities include classrooms, laboratories, a library, sports facilities and computer resources, supported by teaching and administrative staff.

Academic programmes

The college website describes undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across Arts, Commerce, Science, Computer Science and vocational education. Subject areas include Marathi, English, Economics, History, Political Science, Commerce, Physics, Chemistry, Botany, Computer Science, Computer Applications, Software Development, Fashion Technology, Retail Management, Travel and Tourism Management, and Beauty Therapy and Aesthetics. The college also lists doctoral research centres in selected subjects.

Student development

Student development activities listed by the college include NSS, NCC, sports and cultural activities, Vidyarthini Manch, career guidance, placement support, alumni activities, competitive examination guidance and student grievance support.

For the latest course list, admission requirements and notices, visit the official About College page.
